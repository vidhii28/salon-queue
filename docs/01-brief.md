# Salon Queue: Brief

## Problem
On weekends, customers wait 1-2 hours at the salon without knowing
their turn. Some leave, others argue about who came first.

## Users
Customers, receptionist, barbers, owner.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from phone: pick service(s) and barber (or "Any")
2. Live status: position, estimated wait, "You're next!" alert
3. Receptionist dashboard: add walk-ins, call next, mark no-show
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done
5. Waiting-area TV: who is with which barber + next 5 tokens
6. Owner settings: services (time + price), barbers on duty, open/close queue

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp,
loyalty points, multiple branches.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal + gold accent, clean, mobile-first.
Labels in English and Gujarati.
